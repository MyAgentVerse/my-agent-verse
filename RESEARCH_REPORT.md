## PART 2: WHAT $1M–$5M BUSINESSES ACTUALLY BUY

### The 5 Services They Pay For (Ranked by ROI Speed)

**1. Document & Process Automation** ⭐ HIGHEST ROI
- Manual invoice processing: $12–$20/invoice → AI: $2.36/invoice
- Average time savings: 70–80% on document-heavy processes
- ROI timeline: 2–8 weeks
- What they pay: $3,000–$15,000 build + $500–$2,000/month
- Examples: invoice processing, contract review, quoting, permit/compliance docs, job estimates

**2. Operations Workflow Automation** ⭐ HIGHEST DEMAND
- Connects CRM + email + accounting + inventory + scheduling
- Average staff time saved: 8–19 hours/week
- Replaces repetitive Monday-morning tasks that take 3 hours
- What they pay: $5,000–$25,000 build
- Examples: lead-to-quote pipeline, job dispatch, follow-up sequences, reporting

**3. Lead Triage + CRM Automation**
- AI scores, routes, and follows up on inbound leads automatically
- 260% conversion improvement documented in case studies
- What they pay: $5,000–$15,000
- Fast ROI because it directly touches revenue

**4. AI-Assisted Quoting & Proposals**
- Massive underserved gap — especially for field service, contractors, B2B
- Pulls pricing rules + history to generate accurate quotes in minutes not hours
- Owners cite manual quoting as one of their biggest time drains

**5. Support & Ticket Automation**
- Triage, route, and draft responses for customer inquiries
- 40–50% of queries resolved without human touch
- $10,000–$25,000, proven ROI within 60 days

## PART 3: THE PSYCHOLOGY OF THE BUYER

### Who They Are

These are $1M–$5M revenue business owners — usually a founder or operator who:
- Has 5–30 employees
- Is personally doing things they shouldn't be doing
- Knows they need to fix their operations but doesn't have time to research it
- Has been burned by software that promised everything and delivered nothing
- Is skeptical of "AI" hype but secretly desperate for relief

### What Keeps Them Up at Night (Use These Words in Your Copy)

> *"I can't get to the important stuff because I keep getting sucked into the day-to-day."*
> *"My best people are doing admin work."*
> *"We're copying the same data into three different systems."*
> *"I follow up with leads manually and half fall through the cracks."*
> *"We lose jobs because we're slow to respond after hours."*
> *"I'm doing the same thing every Monday morning and it takes three hours."*

**50% of SMB owners say fear of not making enough money keeps them up at night.** Any expenditure needs IMMEDIATE, VISIBLE ROI — not a 12-month projection.

### The Buying Trigger

They buy when they hit a breaking point:
- A lost deal because response was too slow
- A key employee quit and exposed how manual everything was
- They realized they personally worked 70 hours last week
- They did the math on how much they're paying people to copy-paste data

### Language That Converts

✅ **DO use:**
- "Give your team their time back"
- "Stop doing $20/hour work"
- "Your staff should be focused on clients, not spreadsheets"
- "What would you do with 10 extra hours a week?"
- "We set it up. You own it. No ongoing fees to us."
- "Results in 60–90 days"
- "We find 5 ROI-positive automations or your assessment is free"

❌ **DON'T use:**
- "AI-powered solutions"
- "Leverage cutting-edge technology"
- "Transform your business with AI"
- "We implement AI for your business" (too vague)
- Long lists of features
