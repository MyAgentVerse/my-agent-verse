## What We Fix (Common Bottlenecks)

### Lead Response
Lead came in Friday at 4pm. Nobody saw it until Monday. They already booked your competitor.
Fix: Automated instant response via text/email + CRM logging. Live in 2–3 days.

### Follow-Up
One email sent. No reply. You moved on. They bought from whoever sent 7 follow-ups.
Fix: Automated multi-touch follow-up sequence (text + email) that runs until they respond or opt out.

### Sales/Closing
Close rate depends on who picks up the phone. No consistent process.
Fix: Scripted objection handling, automated proposal follow-up, CRM pipeline visibility.

### Delivery/Onboarding
Client signs, nothing happens for 4 days. They email asking what's next. Trust gone.
Fix: Automated onboarding sequence kicks in the moment they sign. Welcome email + next steps + check-in.

### Retention
Job done. Never followed up. Client Googled someone else 6 months later.
Fix: Automated 30/60/90-day check-in sequences and re-engagement campaigns.

### Scheduling Chaos
Dispatch still done by hand. Techs calling in asking where to go.
Fix: Automated dispatch notifications, job confirmations, and route visibility.
